The Latest - Website Feature Inventory

This file lists the main end-user features currently implemented on the site, with a short description of what each feature does.

1. Homepage editorial layout
Presents the main editorial landing experience with hero content, top stories, AI briefing content, and curated content sections.

2. Live news aggregation
Pulls in live news from multiple external feeds and surfaces them as a single browsable news experience.

3. All News browsing
Provides a larger browse view for news articles beyond the homepage highlights.

4. Opinions section
Surfaces editorial, commentary, and opinion-driven content in a dedicated section.

5. Videos section
Collects and displays video content from supported publishers and feeds.

6. Podcasts section
Collects and displays podcast episodes from supported publishers and shows.

7. Social feed aggregation
Shows social posts and platform content alongside traditional news content.

8. Section and category browsing
Lets readers browse by major coverage areas such as politics, business, tech, sports, culture, and lifestyle.

9. Topic pages
Builds topic-specific views that group related content across multiple content types.

10. Search
Allows users to search across site content and discover articles, media, and related topics.

11. Advanced search shortcuts
Provides quick research links and deeper discovery paths for searching beyond the site.

12. AI summary briefing
Generates short AI-assisted summaries of major topics and fast-moving stories.

13. Story clustering
Groups related coverage together so overlapping reporting can be viewed as part of the same story.

14. Perspective comparison
Lets users compare how different outlets are framing the same topic or cluster of stories.

15. Article reader
Provides a dedicated reading view for articles with source context and supporting content.

16. Stable story routes
Creates durable story URLs so articles and story snapshots can be linked and revisited reliably.

17. Saved articles
Lets users bookmark and revisit articles later from a dedicated saved-content view.

18. Reading history
Tracks previously opened articles so readers can return to content they already viewed.

19. Social sharing tools
Makes it easier to share stories externally or copy story links.

20. Source profiles
Provides dedicated profile pages for publishers with ownership, funding, and editorial context.

21. Truth score display
Shows source-level trust context so readers can quickly understand outlet reliability signals.

22. Perspective labels (COMPASS)
Displays editorial-position context such as left-center, center, right-center, or unclassified.

23. User authentication
Supports account sign-in and persistent session-based access to personalized features.

24. Following system
Lets users follow topics, categories, or sources to personalize what they see.

25. Following feed
Builds a personalized feed from the topics and sources a user has chosen to follow.

26. For You recommendations
Surfaces recommendation-style content tailored to a user's follows and behavior.

27. Dashboard
Provides a user or account hub and site-status overview for personalized or administrative workflows.

28. Category filtering
Lets users narrow visible content within major sections and topical collections.

29. Infinite scrolling and large-list browsing
Supports smoother browsing of longer result sets and larger content libraries.

30. Responsive navigation
Includes navigation patterns that work across desktop and mobile layouts.

31. Bottom dock navigation
Provides a mobile-friendly persistent navigation control for key site destinations.

32. Theme toggle
Supports light and dark theme switching for the reading experience.

33. Cookie consent controls
Allows visitors to choose analytics permissions and manage privacy-related consent.

34. Privacy page
Explains data handling, tracking, and privacy practices.

35. Terms of use page
Explains the legal terms that govern use of the site.

36. Editorial standards page
Explains how the site approaches sourcing, labeling, and editorial methodology.

37. Corrections page
Explains how corrections and updates are handled.

38. About page
Introduces the product and its mission.

39. Contact page
Provides readers with a way to contact the team.

40. Advertise page
Provides sponsorship and advertising information.

41. SEO and metadata management
Adds structured metadata, social preview tags, and search-friendly page information.

42. Sitemap and crawler support
Generates sitemap and robots assets to help search engines discover the site.

43. Image optimization
Serves optimized images and fallbacks to improve media performance and reliability.

44. Engagement tracking
Records page and interaction events for analytics when consent allows it.

45. Error boundaries and graceful fallbacks
Prevents isolated UI failures from breaking the full application experience.

46. Date and time utilities
Formats publication times and supports time-aware story presentation throughout the site.
